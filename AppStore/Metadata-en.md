## Description

How was your night? Yawn Sleep turns your sleep data into a small, delightful scene.

A soft 3D bed and the Lil’ Finder Guy react to your personal Yawn Score. The better the night, the tidier the bed and the more refreshed your little companion looks. Different scenes and occasional visits from the Lil’ Finder Lady add variety.

Your Yawn Score at a glance:

• Sleep duration: up to 50 points
• Bedtime consistency: up to 30 points
• Restful sleep with fewer interruptions: up to 20 points

Your score is calculated directly on your device from sleep and wake stages recorded in Apple Health. Yawn Sleep works on iPhone and Apple Watch and requires your explicit permission to read sleep data.

More room on iPhone Duo:

Your morning scene and score breakdown appear side by side on the open display. Narrow displays keep the familiar vertical layout. Yawn Sleep supports portrait and both landscape orientations. Score cards stack at accessibility text sizes, and content scrolls when space is limited.

Look back at your nights:

• Sleep logs for the week, month, and year
• Share summaries as text or PDF and print reports
• Typical bedtime and wake time derived from the last 14 recorded nights
• A local diagnostic report summarizing your score values

Private by design:

• No user account
• No advertising
• No analytics or tracking SDKs
• No upload of your health data
• Processing happens entirely on your devices
• Reports are shared only when you choose to share them

A personal assessment requires recorded sleep data in Apple Health. Without available data, the app shows an illustrative scene with sample values and a Health access message.

The Yawn Score is Yawn Sleep’s independent estimate, not Apple’s Sleep Score or a medical measurement. The app is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, or medical decisions.

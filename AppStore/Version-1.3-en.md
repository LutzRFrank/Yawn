# Yawn Sleep 1.3 – English

## What’s New

Yawn Sleep now makes room for the larger iPhone Duo display.

• Your morning scene and score breakdown appear side by side on the open display.
• Narrow displays keep the familiar vertical layout.
• Support for portrait and both landscape orientations.
• Scrollable content in short windows and stacked score cards at accessibility text sizes.

Your sleep data continues to be processed entirely on your device, with no account, tracking, or advertising.
